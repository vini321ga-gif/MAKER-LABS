## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated precision, utilitarian beauty, and operational clarity required by high-velocity manufacturing and commercial engineering hubs. Drawing inspiration from modern benchmark developer tools and fintech interfaces—specifically Linear, Stripe, and Vercel—the visual ethos balances high-density data handling with airy, serene architectural restraint.

### Target Audience & Tone
Engineers, workshop operators, production planners, and finance directors managing on-demand manufacturing workflows (additive manufacturing, CNC, industrial laser cutting, and UV DTF printing). The interface prioritizes calm authority, tactile feedback, instantaneous legibility, and zero visual clutter.

### Visual Style: Structured Modern Functionalism
- **Micro-Contrast over Heavy Fill:** Depth and separation rely primarily on crisp hairline borders (1px) and muted fills rather than saturated backdrops or theatrical shadows.
- **Instrument-Grade Discipline:** Layouts mimic professional telemetry consoles: monospace/tabular metrics, compact status tags, crisp compartmentalization, and disciplined data alignments.
- **Whitespace as an Active Tool:** Spacious boundaries isolate critical financial ledgers and machine status queues, preventing cognitive fatigue during extended operational shifts.

## Colors

The palette establishes absolute contrast ratios, deliberate semantic hierarchy, and functional calm across high-density reporting views.

### Architectural Canvas & Surfaces
- **App Canvas:** `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`. A tranquil, desaturated ground that avoids the harshness of pure white while reducing screen glare.
- **Surface & Cards:** Pure `#FFFFFF`. Elevates actionable items, tables, and metric cards cleanly above the canvas.
- **Dividers & Hairlines:** `#E2E8F0`. Creates whisper-quiet structural containment.

### Typography & Content
- **Primary / High Emphasize:** Deep Carbon `#0F172A` (titles, lead metrics, active states) and Slate Carbon `#1E293B` (body text).
- **Secondary / Subdued:** Graphite Slate `#64748B` for metadata, column headers, descriptive captions, and empty states.
- **Muted / Placeholder:** `#94A3B8` for disabled states and input placeholders.

### Semantic Instrumentation
- **Success / Revenue (Emerald):** `#10B981` (Core), `#059669` (Dark/Hover), `#ECFDF5` (Soft Fill / Pill BG). Signifies paid orders, revenue inflows, nominal machine calibration, and completed jobs.
- **Destructive / Outflow (Crimson):** `#EF4444` (Core), `#DC2626` (Dark/Hover), `#FEF2F2` (Soft Fill / Pill BG). Marks overhead expenses, printer head failures, stockouts, and cancellations.
- **Attention / Production Pipeline (Amber):** `#F59E0B` (Core), `#D97706` (Dark/Hover), `#FFFBEB` (Soft Fill / Pill BG). Flags pending invoices, UV queue spooling, and warming beds.
- **Informative / Tech Accent (Cobalt Blue):** `#2563EB` (Core), `#3B82F6` (Light Accent), `#EFF6FF` (Soft Fill / Pill BG). Dedicated to primary action cues, selected tabs, active fabrication telemetry, and documentation links.

## Typography

The typography system relies solely on **Inter** to deliver a unified, highly optimized typographic voice.

### Key Rules
- **Tabular Figures for Financials:** Always configure `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` for all monetary entries in Quetzales (e.g., `Q 12,450.00`), table cells, stock counts, and timestamps. This enforces perfect vertical scanning.
- **Labels & Column Headers:** Render `label-sm` in all-caps with generous character spacing (`0.04em`) and medium/semibold weight (`#64748B`) to anchor dense table collections.
- **Display Weights:** Never exceed `font-weight: 600` for titles. The editorial voice is technical and restrained, avoiding blocky or playful extrabolds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances high information density with breathing room, ensuring complex fabrication schedules and P&L sheets stay cleanly partitioned.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fluid 12-column grid bound to a maximum width of `1600px` for ultra-wide displays. Gutter is locked at `1.5rem` (`24px`), section margins at `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Sidebar Architecture:** A fixed 256px collapsible structural sidebar with immediate access to operations (3D Printing Queue, Laser Cutter Telemetry, DTF Inventory, Invoicing).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses into an icon-rail (64px). Gutters shift to `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with standard `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and horizontal screen margin. Critical multi-column financial summaries collapse into stacked card carousels or swipeable horizontal tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as functional stratification rather than ornamentation. The system stays close to the physical surface, favoring ultra-refined hairline boundaries over heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` or `#F1F5F9`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Layered with micro-shadow `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Rows):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Card border transitions subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Menus & Dropdowns):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Command Palette):** Backed by backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) and overlay `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`. Modal container features deep ambient shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometry strikes an exact balance between modern industrial hardware and refined software design:
- **Base Components (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Standard buttons, single-line text inputs, search fields, and dropdown triggers.
- **Containers & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem and `rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Outer data tables, metric tiles, fabrication stage cards, and modal canvases.
- **Tags & Status Badges (Pills):** Full circular caps (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for lifecycle and financial status pills to differentiate them immediately from interactive rectangular controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#1E293B`, border `1px solid transparent`. Height: 36px (compact) or 40px (regular). Text: `13px`, medium weight.
- **Secondary / Neutral:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, hover background `#F8FAFC`. Micro-shadow applied.
- **Accent Action:** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#1D4ED8`. Used for conversion actions (e.g., "Crear Orden de Producción").
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#DC2626`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, hover background `#FEE2E2`.

### Status Badges & Pills
- Always styled with high-contrast text over a tinted background:
  - **Cobrado / Ingreso / Operativo:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, `#A7F3D0` subtle border.
  - **Pendiente / En Producción / Calentando:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text, `#FDE68A` subtle border.
  - **Alerta / Egreso / Mantenimiento:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, `#FECACA` subtle border.
  - **Neutro / Cola de Espera:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, `#E2E8F0` subtle border.
- Include a 6px solid dot indicator beside the status text.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Height: 38px. Background `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Focus State: Border color `#2563EB` with an external 2px focus ring (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).
- Prefix / Suffix adornments: Static background `#F8FAFC` for currency markers (e.g., `Q`) with monospace formatting.

### Cards & Telemetry Tiles
- Background `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-xl`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `1.5rem`.
- Structure: Small uppercase subtitle (`label-sm`), followed by tabular currency or progress value (`metric-currency`), finalized by dynamic delta badge (`+12.4% vs mes anterior`).

### Data Tables (Work Orders & Financials)
- Flush container with header row styled in `#F8FAFC`, uppercase graphite labels, `height: 40px`.
- Row height: 48px to 52px. Row border bottom: `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Hover state: `#F8FAFC`.
- All monetary metrics aligned flush-right with `code-tabular`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Machine Fleet Status Strip:** Segmented progress bar showing runtime status for 3D printers (layer height, bed temp), Laser (tube life, air assist), and UV DTF (ink levels: CMYK+W+Varnish).
- **Quetzal Financial Switcher:** Toggle control to isolate split tax invoices (Factura SAT) vs. standard workshop production slips.
